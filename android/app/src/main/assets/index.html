Real-Time Mobile Notification Capture

How to Auto-Capture Every Phone Payment Notification Into This App

Because mobile operating systems (Android and iOS) sandbox browser apps from reading system-wide notifications in the background without permission, this app includes a built-in URL Query Bridge (`?auto_notif=...`). Whenever your phone receives a Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or bank SMS notification, a tiny local automation rule on your phone can open this app with the notification text—and the app will automatically parse, categorize, and save it to your local storage.

Step 1: Trigger on Payment Alert

In MacroDroid (free on Android) or Tasker, add a Trigger for Notification Received from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Messages containing keywords like debited, paid, or spent.

Step 2: Pass Notification to Local URL

Add an Action: Open Website / HTTP GET and pass the notification body variable into the ?auto_notif= parameter of your saved app file.

Step 3: Add Comment & Export to Excel

The purchase is immediately logged locally in your browser with its auto-detected category. Add your comment anytime and click Export Excel (.xlsx).
